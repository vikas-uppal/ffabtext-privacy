1. Who we are

FFAB Textrend India Private Limited (popularly known as "FFABTEXT", and referred to as "we", "us" or "our") is a pioneer in natural Indian Textiles & a trusted name among garment brands & exporters, fashion designers, premium fabric stores as well as private labels globally. This policy explains what information we handle when we contact businesses by WhatsApp and email, and when you message us. It applies to our WhatsApp Business number and our business email.

2. Information we collect

We deal with businesses, so most of what we hold is business contact information. We collect:

  • Business details: company name, business address, city and country, website and public social-media page (such as Instagram or LinkedIn).
  • Business contact details: business phone and WhatsApp number, business email address, and, where it is publicly listed, the name and job title of a contact person.
  • What you send us: your replies to our messages, your answers to our short qualification questions, your button taps (for example "Yes, happy to answer", "Not now" or "Stop messages"), and anything you choose to tell us.
  • Message records: the date and time messages were sent, delivered and read, and your WhatsApp profile name and number as shown to us by WhatsApp.
  • Your choices: a record if you ask us to stop contacting you, so we honour it.

We do not ask for passwords, payment card details or government ID numbers by WhatsApp or email, and you should not send them.

3. Where we get it

  • Publicly available sources, such as business websites, online business directories, Google search results and public social-media business profiles.
  • You, when you reply to us, visit our showroom, speak with our team or send us an enquiry.

We choose the businesses we contact because their products suggest they may use natural fabrics. We do not buy personal data lists.

4. How we use it

  • To introduce FFABTEXT and share our company deck and fabric catalogue.
  • To ask short questions about your business so we can tell whether our fabrics suit you.
  • To arrange a call or a visit, and to answer your questions.
  • To keep records of our contact with you, avoid contacting you twice about the same thing, and honour opt-outs.
  • To improve how we reach out, using summary numbers such as replies and opt-outs.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Our basis for using it

Where the law requires a basis, we rely on our legitimate interest in offering our products to businesses that may want them, balanced against your rights and expectations, and on your consent when you reply or tap a button to continue the conversation. You can withdraw that at any time (see section 7).

6. WhatsApp and who else sees your information

We send WhatsApp messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. and its group companies. Messages pass through Meta's systems, and Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policy, which you can read at WhatsApp.com/legal and facebook.com/privacy/policy. We have no control over how Meta uses the information it handles.

We also use:

  • Google Drive, to host the company deck and catalogue we link to. The folder is shared for viewing.
  • Email and cloud storage providers, to send email and hold our contact records.
  • Our own staff, who handle replies and make the calls and visits we offer.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights. We do not share your information with others for their own marketing.

Because we are in India and contact businesses in other countries, your information may be processed in India and in other countries where our providers operate.

7. Stopping messages and your choices

You can stop us contacting you at any time, and we will act on it promptly:

  • Tap the "Stop messages" button on our first WhatsApp message, or reply STOP.
  • Email us at connect@ftipl.com with the word "unsubscribe" and the number or address to remove.

After that we will not message you again, and we keep only the minimum record needed to remember your request.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Email connect@ftipl.com and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Deleting your data

To have your information deleted, email connect@ftipl.com from the address or number you want removed, or from your business email, with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete the information we hold, except what we must keep by law and a short opt-out record so we do not contact you again. We will confirm by email when it is done.

9. How long we keep it

We keep business contact records while we may reasonably still be in touch with you and delete or anonymise them within 24 months after our last interaction, unless you have become a customer, in which case we keep records as long as needed for the relationship and for tax and legal reasons. Opt-out records are kept so that we honour them.

10. Security

We limit access to our contact records to staff who need them, protect our accounts with passwords and access controls, and keep access tokens private. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a material change, we will update this page.
